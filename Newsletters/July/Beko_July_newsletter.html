Housebuilders could be missing a critical factor in energy efficiency. Insulation. Airtightness. Clean energy. As the Future Homes Standard becomes a reality, these are rightly front of mind. But there's another part of the home that can have a significant impact on long-term energy performance: the appliance set, and how people use it.

Here's why.

Around 27% of electricity in UK homes is consumed by refrigeration and laundry appliances.** Getting the most from them relies on everyday habits like choosing eco programmes, running full loads and carrying out simple maintenance.

The products play a key role, too. Appliances designed to be repaired and refurbished, rather than replaced, can help reduce waste, lower embodied carbon and extend the life of the home's specification.

So while homeowner behaviour isn't something housebuilders can influence, appliance choice is.

By specifying efficient, durable appliances from the outset, you can give buyers a better starting point. Products that are affordable to run, built to last and supported throughout their lifetime help homes continue performing long after handover.
So what can housebuilders do?

While consumer behaviour might feel out of the housebuilder's hands, there are real ways you can help.
  • Spec for sustainability - offer an efficient appliance set designed with sustainability considerations in mind.
  • Make sustainability accessible - efficiency shouldn't be a luxury, so give buyers affordable, desirable options that perform.
  • Deliver durability - select appliances built for repairability and refurbishment, with long product life, flexible warranties and strong aftercare.
