Great homeowner experiences don't end at handover
Handing over the keys is an important milestone, but it's only the beginning of the homeowner journey. What follows is just as important, with every interaction helping to shape how homeowners feel about their new home.

The Home Builders Federation (HBF) Customer Satisfaction Survey reflects this wider journey, measuring homeowner satisfaction at both eight weeks and nine months after completion. It recognises that homeowner experience is influenced by every touchpoint post-handover - from customer care and warranty support to the partners who help homeowners settle into their new home.

That includes appliance support.

Whether homeowners are registering their appliances, looking for product guidance or seeking technical assistance, responsive support helps create a seamless ownership experience while minimising disruption. Every positive interaction helps build confidence and reinforces the quality of the home their housebuilder has delivered.
