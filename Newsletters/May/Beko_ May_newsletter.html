Multi-brand innovation, as seen at EuroCucina 2026
Did you make it to EuroCucina in April? The event provided the perfect platform to showcase our vision for future kitchen living. Demonstrating how intelligent technology, seamless design and connected experiences can help create homes that work harder for both developers and homeowners.

At the heart of the showcase was our flexible multi-brand ecosystem, bringing together Whirlpool, Beko and Hotpoint to deliver tailored kitchen solutions across a range of property types and buyer needs. Connecting the experience is HomeWhiz, our unified smart home platform, which gives homeowners greater visibility and control over appliance usage, energy and water consumption. By transforming data into actionable insights, HomeWhiz helps households build more efficient routines while supporting lower running costs and a better ownership experience.

Together, these innovations demonstrate how connected appliances can go beyond convenience - helping create more desirable, efficient and future-ready homes.
